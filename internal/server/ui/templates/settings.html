The zone every node answers, such as mesh or home.arpa. Changing it renames every node's name at once: nas.{{.DNSZone}} becomes nas. and the new zone.

Domains the mesh takes over, comma separated, such as example.lan: every node answers the containers' names in them (hostname plus domainname, aliases) and sends every other name in them to the upstream resolvers, and the hosts send the whole domain to the mesh -- not to their own resolver any more.
